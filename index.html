About the log
Same-window, same-side bets are grouped. Each chip is one account's P/L and stake. Fill = share of the bet the real order book had within 2¢ of the entry price when the bot bought. "max" = the biggest bet that would have filled 100% within 2¢ (≥ means the book showed more than we recorded). "Fully fills 80% of the time up to $X" in Details is the bet size that would have filled completely within 2¢ on about 4 of 5 trades. "Realistic (fills within 2¢)" walks the recorded book up to 2¢ past the entry price, counts that fill (and its slippage and fees), and uses the original P/L when there is no order-book snapshot (before the Nov 18 recorder).
